## Silver Valkyrie
- **Silver Acceleration:** Attack wins add +5% base ATK per Speed stack, up to 4; an Attack loss removes 1.
- **Valkyrie’s Edge:** Two consecutive Attack wins arm the next normal Attack to ignore 20% enemy DEF.
- **Silver Wing Breaker:** 1 hit(s), each 150 base + 45% ATTACK. Consume Speed stacks for +12 damage each. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Arc Bahamut
- **Arc Barrier:** Start with a 15% max HP shield. When it breaks, gain +15% base DEF for 2 rounds.
- **Recoil Vault:** Store 20% of shield-absorbed damage, capped at 60; release it as bonus counter damage on your next Defense win.
- **Bahamut Fortress:** 1 hit(s), each 110 base + 40% DEFENSE. Replace your Arc shield with a 20% max HP shield lasting 2 rounds; replacement does not trigger breaking. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Circle Bahamut
- **Circular Guard:** Defense wins grant 1 Orbit stack, up to 3; each reduces incoming damage by 4%. An Attack loss removes 1.
- **Unbroken Circle:** Start a round with 3 Orbit stacks to prevent incoming Stability damage that round. Action costs still apply. Consume those stacks at round end.
- **Ring of Judgment:** 1 hit(s), each 125 base + 45% DEFENSE. Restore 12 Stability and gain 2 Orbit stacks, up to 3. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Judgement Joker
- **Loaded Verdict:** Attack wins earn a Red token; Defense wins earn a Black token. Hold up to 2 of each; Specials earn no tokens.
- **Double Jeopardy:** Your next normal Attack consumes one token of each colour, if available, for +15% damage and an 8% max HP shield.
- **Final Verdict:** 1 hit(s), each 140 base + 35% ATTACK. Consume all tokens: +15 damage per Red and +5% max HP shield per Black. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Jail Jormungand
- **Heavy Landing:** Defense wins reduce enemy base ATK by 8% for 2 rounds. Repeated activation refreshes instead of stacking.
- **Crushing Weight:** Above 60% HP, successful Defense counters gain bonus damage equal to 15% of base DEF.
- **Giant Hammerfall:** 1 hit(s), each 130 base + 55% DEFENSE. If your Heavy Landing debuff is active, extend it by 1 round and restore 8 Stability. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Greatest Raphael
- **Halo Ascension:** Gain 1 Halo stack at each round end, up to 4; each grants +3% base ATK and DEF.
- **Angelic Reversal:** Once per battle when damage leaves you below 40% HP, remove negative stat modifiers and gain 20% damage reduction for the next 2 rounds. Damage over time remains.
- **Greatest Halo:** 1 hit(s), each 145 base + 40% ATTACK. Consume Halo stacks to heal 3% max HP per stack; stacks can rebuild. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Bushin Ashura
- **Six-Armed Guard:** Defense wins grant 1 Guard arm, up to 3; your next incoming normal Attack consumes all arms for 7% damage reduction each.
- **Bushin Discipline:** Use 3 different normal actions consecutively to restore 6 Stability and gain +12% base DEF for 2 rounds. Repeated actions reset the sequence; Specials interrupt it.
- **Ashura’s Sixfold Strike:** 6 hit(s), each 20 base + 8% DEFENSE. Six hits, each 20 base + 8% DEF; resolve defense separately. Does not trigger normal Attack abilities. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Orb Engaard
- **Orb Deflection:** Every third enemy normal Attack deals 30% less damage. Specials do not advance this counter.
- **Perfect Sphere:** When both fighters choose Defense, restore 5 Stability and gain 1 Sphere charge, up to 3.
- **Engaard Repulsion:** 1 hit(s), each 120 base + 40% DEFENSE. Consume Sphere charges for 8% damage reduction each during the next round. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Ace Dragon
- **Dragon Pursuit:** Attack losses grant 1 Pursuit stack, up to 3; the next Attack win consumes them for +10% damage each.
- **Ace Momentum:** After Charge, your next normal Attack within 2 rounds gains bonus damage equal to 15% base ATK. Does not stack or boost Specials.
- **Dragonic Ace:** 1 hit(s), each 155 base + 45% ATTACK. Consume Pursuit stacks to pierce 5% Defense mitigation each. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

Bundled parts use the existing 45% / 27.5% / 27.5% split and remain outside the shop. Bonuses use levelled base battle stats; Specials use the shared live-stat formula. Cooldowns use the shared Special gate. New mechanics require the shared BattleSession engine (PvP and Story); the separate boss projection reports them as dormant rather than claiming full support.
