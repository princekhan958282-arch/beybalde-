# Four approved Burst Beys

Supplied names, types, artwork links and approved abilities are preserved. Rarity and stat lines are proposed roster balance. New records use BB160–BB163.

| Hyperion Burn Cho Xceed | Attack | Ultimate | 150 | 170 | 80 | 120 |

## Black Especially
- **Shadow Adaptation:** An Attack loss grants +12% base DEF for 2 rounds; a Defense loss grants +12% base ATK for 2 rounds. Each bonus refreshes instead of stacking.
- **Eclipse Seal:** After Charge, your first successful normal Attack seals the enemy for 2 rounds, reducing their healing by 25%. Seals do not stack.
- **Black Eclipse:** 1 hit(s), each 145 base + 25% ATTACK + 25% DEFENSE. Consume an active Eclipse Seal for +35 damage. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Grand Dragon
- **Dragon Excavation:** Successful normal Attacks deal 50% extra shield-only damage. Unused extra damage never overflows into HP.
- **Grand Breakthrough:** Breaking an enemy shield arms your next normal Attack for +25% damage. Cannot stack or boost the shield-breaking hit itself.
- **Grand Dragon Crusher:** 1 hit(s), each 160 base + 50% ATTACK. Deal 40 extra shield-only damage before resolving the normal hit. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Heaven Pegasus
- **Heaven’s Recovery:** Stamina wins grant 1 Feather, up to 3. Each Feather increases Stamina-action HP healing by 5%.
- **Pegasus Slipstream:** After a Stamina win, your next Charge within 2 rounds steals up to 0.8 enemy stamina and restores that amount to you. Cannot stack.
- **Heaven Ascension:** 1 hit(s), each 125 base + 50% STAMINA. Consume Feathers to restore 1 stamina and 4 Stability per Feather. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

## Hyperion Burn Cho Xceed
- **Burn Engine:** Successful normal Attacks generate 1 Heat, up to 4. Using Stamina removes 1 Heat. Heat itself grants no stat bonus.
- **Xceed Ignition:** Charge with at least 2 Heat consumes exactly 2 and arms your next successful normal Attack for +30% damage. Expires after 2 rounds; cannot stack or boost Specials.
- **Super Hyperion Burn:** 3 hit(s), each 55 base + 18% ATTACK. Consume remaining Heat for +6 damage per Heat on each hit. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

All bonus damage uses the standard damage pipeline. Grand Dragon shield-only damage never overflows into HP. Stamina stealing is limited to Heaven Pegasus. New effects use the shared PvP/Story engine; the separate boss projection reports them as dormant. Bundled parts remain outside the shop.
