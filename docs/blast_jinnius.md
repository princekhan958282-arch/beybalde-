# Blast Jinnius

BB164 • Defense • Legendary • Burst • Right spin

HP 155 / ATK 85 / DEF 155 / STM 110 / Special 140.

- **Cyclone Barrier:** all negative stability deltas are reduced by 25%, retaining at least 1 loss. Charge restores 6 Stability, with a 2-round cooldown on recovery.
- **Vortex Pressure:** Defense wins remove 8 enemy Stability and restore 4 own Stability; 2-round cooldown.
- **Blast Cyclone:** one hit, 140 base + 55% DEF. Remove 22 enemy Stability, or 30 when the enemy begins the Special below 35% of their own maximum Stability. Restore 12 own Stability. Cooldown: 4 rounds.

Recovery clamps to the owner's stability maximum. Stability damage uses the shared StabilityManager, including resistance and ring-out checks. The conditional Special bonus is evaluated before the base stability hit, so the Special cannot qualify itself by crossing the threshold. Casting during cooldown applies no secondary effects.

The supplied PNG URL is retained. The bundled Cyclone Disk and Tower Driver are hidden from the shop, with default component IDs and the normal 45% / 27.5% / 27.5% stat distribution.
