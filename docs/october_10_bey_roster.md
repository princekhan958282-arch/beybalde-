Shelter Regulus Defense Mode: HP 140 / ATK 75 / DEF 130 / STM 110. It uses Shelter Shield, Regulus Guard, and Shelter Lion Fortress (150 base). Both modes spin right; the existing mode picker supports non-spin forms.

Deathscyth Longinus uses the existing independent 1-in-10,000 natural-spawn roll and is excluded from ordinary weighted spawns. It is not booster exclusive.

Each new Bey has a bundled Disk and Driver, with the existing 45% / 27.5% / 27.5% stat split and deterministic rounding. Bundled parts are not sold in the shop.

## Golden Imperial Dragon

- **Imperial Crown:** Start battle with a shield equal to 12% of maximum HP.
- **Sovereign Recovery:** Every Stamina win restores 5% of maximum HP.

## Black Valkyrie

- **Night Wing:** Successful Attacks gain bonus damage equal to 10% of ATK.
- **Midnight Resolve:** Once per battle below 35% HP, gain 18% ATK for 2 rounds.

## Black Brave Valkyrie

- **Brave Reprisal:** After an Attack loss, gain 15% ATK for 2 rounds. Cooldown: 3 rounds.
- **Obsidian Courage:** Once per battle below 40% HP, gain a shield equal to 15% of maximum HP.

## Ultimate Dark Valkyrie

- **Eclipse Edge:** Successful Attacks gain bonus damage equal to 14% of ATK.
- **Dark Ascension:** Each Special restores 6 Stability.

## Strike Longinus

- **Lance Breaker:** Successful Attacks against Defense gain 18% bonus damage.
- **Impact Anchor:** Each Attack win restores 4 Stability.

## Dragon Circle

- **Endless Orbit:** Each Stamina win steals 0.5 enemy stamina.
- **Circle Renewal:** Each Charge restores 4% of maximum HP.

## Legend Spriggan

- **Legend Counter:** Each Defense win restores 5 Stability.
- **Crossfire Momentum:** Every Special grants 12% ATK for 2 rounds.

## Shelter Regulus

- **Lion Claw:** Successful Attacks gain bonus damage equal to 12% of ATK.
- **Pouncing Balance:** Every Charge restores 4 Stability.

## Abyss Fang

- **Razor Hunger:** Successful Attacks gain bonus damage equal to 12% of ATK.
- **Last Bite:** Once per battle below 30% HP, recover 10% of maximum HP.

## Deathscyth Longinus

- **Reaper Mark:** Successful Attacks gain 15% bonus damage against enemies below 40% HP.
- **Soul Armor:** Once per battle below 35% HP, gain a shield equal to 12% of maximum HP.
