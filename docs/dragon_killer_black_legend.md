# S,Dragon Killer and Black Legend

Both are separate stock Beys with their supplied image URLs, two abilities per form, a Special, and bundled Disk/Driver parts. Parts use the existing 45% / 27.5% / 27.5% split and remain unavailable in the shop. Stats and Black Legend's Legendary rarity follow the user's authorization to create missing kit details.

| Bey / mode | Rarity | Type | HP | ATK | DEF | STM | Special base |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| S,Dragon Killer | Ultimate | Attack | 155 | 180 | 65 | 125 | 195 (3 × 65) |
| Black Legend — Attack | Legendary | Attack | 145 | 155 | 80 | 110 | 160 (4 × 40) |
| Black Legend — Counter | Legendary | Defense | 145 | 80 | 155 | 110 | 160 (2 × 80) |

S,Dragon Killer also accepts `S Dragon Killer` and `S, Dragon Killer` in lookups.

- **Dragonbane Edge:** successful Attacks gain 12% ATK as bonus damage.
- **Dragon Slayer:** Special gains 8% of current enemy HP once on the first hit.
- **Obsidian Slash (Attack):** successful Attacks gain 10% ATK as bonus damage.
- **Black Rally (Attack):** once below 30% HP, gain 15% ATK for 2 rounds.
- **Obsidian Reversal (Counter):** while using Defense, reflect 20% of incoming normal Attack damage. Does not trigger on Specials.
- **Counter Renewal (Counter):** Defense wins heal 6% of maximum HP.

Black Legend's forms are selected through the existing `;info` mode buttons and persisted by the existing profile mode system. Attack is the default. Both modes spin right. The stat change is intrinsic to the frame; equipped Disk/Driver contributions remain correct when changing modes or replacing parts.

Supplied artwork links are retained. Both returned HTTP 403 from the development environment, so local artwork could not be downloaded or visually verified.
